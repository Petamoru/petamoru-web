At a glance

  • We process account data, technical data and the records you create about your pets’ care.
  • The database is hosted in the European Union. Some provider services may involve processing outside the European Economic Area.
  • The app has no ads. We don’t use cross-app or cross-site tracking tools or sell your data.
  • You can request account deletion in the app or by email.

2. What data we process

Account data

  • If you sign up by email: your email address, display name and password. Only a password hash is stored.
  • If you sign in with Google: the email, name and Google account identifier Google provides with your authorisation.
  • If you use guest mode: a guest identifier without requiring you to sign up with an email address. That identifier can link data to your session, so guest mode does not guarantee anonymity.

What you record in the app

  • Households you create or join, your role in each household, and join requests and invitation codes.
  • Pet and care data, including names, species and other profile details, medication schedules and doses, meals, walks, weights, notes, vaccinations and vet appointments.
  • Who made each entry and when, so other household members can see it.

Please avoid including sensitive personal data, such as a person’s health information, in notes or other free-text fields.

Technical data

  • Notification token: an identifier assigned to your device so it can receive updates, for example to clear a reminder after someone else has given a dose.
  • IP address: we use it to limit invitation-code attempts and protect households from misuse. Our providers also record it in technical security logs.
  • Crash reports: if the app unexpectedly closes or encounters an error, a report containing your device model, operating system and app versions, and technical error details is sent with an installation identifier and your internal user identifier, a random code that does not reveal who you are. These reports do not include your email, name or the contents of your entries.

What we don’t collect

We don’t access your location, contacts, photos or microphone. We don’t use advertising or cross-app or cross-site tracking tools.

Data on your device

The app stores a copy of your household data on your device for offline use. In guest mode, data is stored on your device and on our servers under your guest identifier.

5. How long we keep your data

  • Account: account data is kept while you maintain the account. See the deletion page for what account deletion covers.
  • Shared household records: if you delete your account, your entries in households with other members are retained to preserve the pets’ care history. They appear under “Former member” instead of your name. People who took part in the household may still recognise you from the context or the entries themselves. Households where you were the only member are included in account deletion.
  • Guest mode: a guest that has not used the app for 30 days is deleted automatically, together with its household.
  • Notification tokens: deleted when they have not been refreshed for 60 days, or as soon as the notification service reports them as invalid.
  • Invitation codes and failed attempts: codes are deleted 24 hours after they expire or are revoked, and failed attempts 24 hours after they happen. Join requests expire after 7 days.
  • Crash reports: kept for up to 90 days.

Technical backups held by our database provider may contain data until they are overwritten in their regular cycle.

6. Your rights

You can exercise your rights to access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction of processing and data portability:

  • Delete your account and data: use Profile → Delete account in the app (Delete my data in guest mode) or write to us. See the deletion instructions.
  • Change your display name: open Profile in the app.
  • Request a copy of your data or exercise another right: use Profile → Request my data (GDPR) or email privacy@petamoru.com from your account email address. We will tell you what action we have taken without undue delay and normally within one month. If an extension applies under the GDPR, we will tell you within that first month.

You can lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD) if you believe we have mishandled your data. You are also welcome to write to us so we can try to resolve the issue.

7. Security

Communications between the app and our servers are encrypted. Server queries are restricted to the households you belong to, and passwords are stored as hashes. No system is infallible, but we apply technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risk.

8. Children

Petamoru is not directed at children under 14. If you are under 14, you need consent from a parent or guardian to create an account. If we learn that we collected data from a child under 14 without that consent, we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date above. If the change is significant, we will notify you in the app before it takes effect.
