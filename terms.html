2. The service

Petamoru lets you create households, add pets and invite others to record and review care together. This includes medication schedules and doses, meals, walks, weight, notes, vaccinations and vet appointments, with reminders and syncing across devices.

The service is free and does not include ads. While it is in beta, features may change, fail or be removed.

3. Petamoru is not a veterinary service

  • The app is an organisation tool. It does not provide diagnoses, clinical assessments or veterinary advice. Data and documents it generates have no official or legal validity.
  • You or other household members enter schedules, doses and dates. Always check them against your vet’s instructions.
  • Reminders and possible duplicate-dose alerts can be missed or delayed due to device settings such as permissions, battery saver or Do Not Disturb, poor connectivity or technical problems.
  • Contact a vet with any concern about your pet’s health or in an emergency.

4. Your account

  • To use Petamoru, you must be at least 14 years old or have consent from a parent or guardian.
  • You can sign up with email and a password or with Google. Provide accurate information and keep your credentials private. You are responsible for activity on your account.
  • Guest mode lets you try the app without signing up with an email address, with some limitations. A guest that has not used the app for 30 days is deleted automatically, together with its data. Create an account if you want to keep using that data.

5. Households and members

  • Whoever creates a household is its administrator. Administrators generate invitation codes, accept or reject join requests and manage members, roles and pets.
  • Household members can see everything you record there, along with your display name. Invite only people you trust and keep your invitation code private.
  • If you leave or are removed from a household, your entries are kept with your name to preserve the pets’ care history. If you delete your account, entries in households with other members are retained under “Former member”. Other members may still recognise you from the context or the entries themselves.
