How it works

From the first invite to the last dose

01

Bring your household together

Add your pets and invite whoever looks after them with a code. You approve each request and decide who can manage the household.

02

Log it in two taps

Mark a dose as given or skipped when you give it. Meals, walks, weight and notes are just as quick, even without coverage.

03

Doubles get caught

If two people give the same dose, Petamoru flags a possible double dose on both phones so you can sort it out together.

04

Everything stays in the timeline

Doses, meals, walks and vet visits, with who logged each one. Filter by pet, type or person.
