Request deletion from the app

Open Petamoru and go to Profile → Delete account, or Profile → Delete my data in guest mode. Follow the instructions shown in the app.

Request deletion by email

You can request deletion without opening the app. Write to privacy@petamoru.com with the subject “Petamoru account deletion”.

  1. Say that you want to delete your account and its associated data. If possible, send the request from your account email address, including if you sign in with Google.
  2. If you can’t access that email address or you use guest mode, explain that in your message so we can follow up. Keep passwords and verification codes private.

We may need to verify that the request is yours before processing it. We complete email requests within 30 days.

What happens to your data

  • Account deletion covers your account details and households where you were the only member.
  • Entries in households with other members remain in the pets’ care history under “Former member”. Others may still recognise you from the context or the entries themselves.
  • Technical backups held by our database provider may contain data until they are overwritten in their regular cycle.

For details on retention and your rights, see the privacy policy.
